# Defenses (Shields & Armor)

Defenses are items that protect your infrastructure from attacks through blocking, damage reduction, or other defensive mechanics.

## Basic Defenses

### Firewall
- **Shape:** 1x2 (2 slots vertical)
- **Block Chance:** 30%
- **Block Amount:** 10
- **CPU Steal:** 0.7 per block
- **Cost:** 5 Gold
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Class:** Neutral
- **Description:** Network defense that blocks attacks and steals CPU
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Wooden Buckler

### Proxy Shield
- **Shape:** 1x1 (1 slot)
- **Block Chance:** 25%
- **Block Amount:** 8
- **Effect:** Adjacent items gain +5% block chance
- **Cost:** 4 Gold
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Class:** Neutral
- **Description:** Redirects attacks through proxy layers
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Stone Skin Potion effect

### Barbed Firewall
- **Shape:** 1x1 (1 slot)
- **Block Chance:** 35%
- **Block Amount:** 12
- **Effect:** 50% chance to reflect 3 damage on successful block
- **Cost:** 8 Gold
- **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **Class:** Neutral
- **Description:** Advanced firewall using quantum encryption
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Spiked Shield

### Cipher Lock
- **Shape:** 2x2 (4 slots square)
- **Block Chance:** 40%
- **Block Amount:** 15
- **Effect:** Reduces all incoming damage by 2
- **Cost:** 12 Gold
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Class:** Neutral
- **Description:** Heavy encryption that reduces all damage
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Heavy Armor

### Heatsink Wall
- **Shape:** 2x1 (2 slots horizontal)
- **Block Chance:** 30%
- **Block Amount:** 10
- **Effect:** On block: 30% chance to freeze attacker for 1s
- **Cost:** 7 Gold
- **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **Class:** Neutral
- **Description:** Ultra-cold defense that can freeze attackers
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Frost Shield

## Sentaur Class Defenses

### Mesh Vest
- **Shape:** 2x1 (2 slots horizontal)
- **Block Chance:** 25%
- **Block Amount:** 6
- **Effect:** Gains 1 adaptation stack on successful block (max 5). Each stack: +4% damage reduction
- **Cost:** 6 Gold
- **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **Class:** Sentaur
- **Description:** Defensive system that evolves with each encounter
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Leather Armor
