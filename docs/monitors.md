alis# Monitors (Armor)

Monitors are the equivalent of "Armor" from Backpack Battles. They provide defensive capabilities and damage mitigation.

## Rare Monitors

### Basic Firewall
- **Shape:** 2x2 (4 slots square)
- **Effect:** +15 Block
- **Cost:** 7 Gold
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Description:** Standard network protection
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Leather Armor

## Epic Monitors

### Cryogenic Shield
- **Shape:** 2x2 (4 slots square)
- **Effect:** +30 Block. Opponent is inflicted with 1 Cold each time they hit you
- **Cost:** 11 Gold
- **Rarity:** Epic
- **Description:** Freezing cold slows attackers
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Ice Armor

## Legendary Monitors

### Sanctified Firewall
- **Shape:** 2x2 (4 slots square)
- **Effect:** +45 Block. Start of battle: Cleanse 5 debuffs
- **Cost:** 13 Gold
- **Rarity:** Legendary
- **Description:** Blessed protection that purifies the system
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Holy Armor

## Godly Monitors

### Corrupted Kernel
- **Shape:** 2x2 (4 slots square)
- **Effect:** +70 Block. You are inflicted with 5 Bugs. Adjacent Exploits gain +8 damage
- **Cost:** 20 Gold
- **Rarity:** Godly
- **Description:** Powerful but unstable kernel modification
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Corrupted Armor
