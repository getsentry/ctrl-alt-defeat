# Consumables (Limited Use Items)

Consumables are items that have limited uses or are consumed upon activation, providing powerful but temporary effects.

## Basic Consumables

### Health Potion
- **Shape:** 1x1 (1 slot)
- **Effect:** When health drops below 50%: Restore 15-20 health and consume item
- **Cost:** 3 Gold
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Class:** Neutral
- **Description:** Emergency health restoration
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Health Potion

### Energy Drink
- **Shape:** 1x1 (1 slot)
- **Effect:** Battle start: +10 max CPU for battle, then consumed
- **Cost:** 4 Gold
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Class:** Neutral
- **Description:** Temporary CPU boost
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Mana Potion

### Smoke Bomb
- **Shape:** 1x1 (1 slot)
- **Effect:** When health drops below 30%: Grant 50% dodge chance for 3s, then consumed
- **Cost:** 5 Gold
- **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **Class:** Neutral
- **Description:** Emergency evasion tool
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Smoke Bomb

## Sentaur Class Consumables

### Packet Burst
- **Shape:** 1x1 (1 slot)
- **Stackable:** Yes (max 5 stacks)
- **Effect:** +2 damage per stack on attack. Consumes 1 stack per attack
- **Cost:** 2 Gold
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Class:** Sentaur
- **Description:** Stackable ammunition that enhances firepower
- **Not offered:** its source is a Hunter subclass item, and subclasses are not built
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Piercing Arrow

### Rubber Duck
- **Shape:** 2x1 (2 slots horizontal)
- **Effect:** Battle start: Spawns AI Tactical Drone (30 HP, 3-5 damage/2s, +15% accuracy to allies, heals 2 HP/4s)
- **Cost:** 15 Gold
- **Rarity:** Epic
- **Class:** Sentaur
- **Description:** Autonomous assistant that provides tactical support
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Carrot Goobert

### Telemetry Drone
- **Shape:** 1x1 (1 slot)
- **Effect:** Battle start: Spawns defensive drone (10 HP, 25% block chance, blocks 5 damage)
- **Cost:** 4 Gold
- **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **Class:** Sentaur
- **Description:** Defensive companion that blocks incoming attacks
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Hedgehog

### Web Crawler
- **Shape:** 1x1 (1 slot)
- **Effect:** Battle start: Spawns fast attacker (8 HP, 2 damage/1.5s)
- **Cost:** 4 Gold
- **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **Class:** Sentaur
- **Description:** Fast-attacking companion that gathers intelligence
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Rat

### Cache Spider
- **Shape:** 1x1 (1 slot)
- **Effect:** Battle start: Spawns resource gatherer (6 HP, +0.5 CPU/s)
- **Cost:** 4 Gold
- **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **Class:** Sentaur
- **Description:** Resource-gathering companion that boosts efficiency
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Squirrel

### Repair Swarm
- **Shape:** 1x1 (1 slot)
- **Effect:** When health drops below 40%: Restore 12-18 health and consume item
- **Cost:** 3 Gold
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Class:** Sentaur
- **Description:** Nano-repair swarm for emergency health restoration
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Healing Herbs

### Restore Point
- **Shape:** 1x1 (1 slot)
- **Effect:** When health drops below 30%: Restore 20-25 health, gain 20% damage reduction for 5s, consume item
- **Cost:** 5 Gold
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Class:** Sentaur
- **Description:** Full system restoration protocol
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Carrot

## Notes

- Consumable items provide powerful effects but are limited use
- Stack mechanics for ammunition items like Packet Burst
- Companion items spawn autonomous units that fight alongside you
- Class-specific consumables synergize with their respective class items
