# Patches (Potions)

Patches are the equivalent of "Potions" from Backpack Battles. They are one-time use items with immediate effects.

## Rare Patches

### Hotfix Ampoule
- **Shape:** 1x1 (1 slot)
- **Effect:** Infrastructure drops below 50%: Deploy this and restore 12 Infrastructure and clear 4 Bugs
- **Cost:** 4 Gold
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Description:** Quick patch for critical system failures
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Health Potion

## Epic Patches

### Swap Shot
- **Shape:** 1x1 (1 slot)
- **Effect:** Memory used or Infrastructure drops below 50%: Deploy this and gain 4 Memory and 18 maximum Infrastructure
- **Cost:** 6 Gold
- **Rarity:** Epic
- **Description:** Injects additional memory when resources are low
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Mana Potion

### Hardening Serum
- **Shape:** 1x1 (1 slot)
- **Effect:** 45 Block reached: Deploy this and convert 15 Infrastructure to 30 Block
- **Cost:** 6 Gold
- **Rarity:** Epic
- **Description:** Sacrifices infrastructure for enhanced security
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Stone Skin Potion

### Payload Vial
- **Shape:** 1x1 (1 slot)
- **Effect:** Opponent repairs: Deploy this and inflict 3 Malware and 1 Malware to yourself
- **Cost:** 7 Gold
- **Rarity:** Epic
- **Description:** Risky patch that spreads malware to both systems
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Pestilence Flask

## Legendary Patches

### Overclock Shot
- **Shape:** 1x1 (1 slot)
- **Effect:** Out of CPU: Deploy this and regenerate 2 CPU and gain 1 Overclock
- **Cost:** 6 Gold
- **Rarity:** Legendary
- **Description:** Emergency performance enhancement
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Heroic Potion

## Godly Patches

### Restore Point
- **Shape:** 1x1 (1 slot)
- **Effect:** You reached 10 errors: Deploy this and clear 10 errors
- **Cost:** 7 Gold
- **Rarity:** Godly
- **Description:** Complete system rollback to clear all errors
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Divine Potion
