# Scripts (Food)

Scripts are the equivalent of "Food" from Backpack Battles. They provide healing and ongoing buffs.

## Common Scripts

### Health Check Script
- **Shape:** 2x1 (2 slots horizontal)
- **Effect:** Every 5s: Restore 4 Infrastructure and regenerate 1 CPU
- **Cost:** 3 Gold
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Description:** Automated health monitoring that repairs damage
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Banana

### Firewall Script
- **Shape:** 1x1 (1 slot)
- **Effect:** Every 4s: Gain 3 Block. 30% chance to remove 1 Data Theft from opponent
- **Cost:** 2 Gold
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Description:** Basic security script that blocks attacks
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Garlic

## Rare Scripts

### Cache Optimizer
- **Shape:** 1x1 (1 slot)
- **Effect:** Every 3.5s: Gain 1 Memory. If you have at least 10 Memory: gain 1 Performance instead
- **Cost:** 2 Gold
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Description:** Optimizes memory usage for better performance
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Blueberries

## Legendary Scripts

### Load Balancer Script
- **Shape:** 2x2 (4 slots square)
- **Effect:** Every 3.3s: Gain 1 Deflection and restore 4 Infrastructure
- **Cost:** 7 Gold
- **Rarity:** Legendary
- **Description:** Distributes incoming traffic to prevent overload
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Pineapple

## Unique Scripts

### AI Assistant Bot
- **Shape:** 3x2 (6 slots)
- **Effect:** Starts with 40 max health, uses resources to gain buffs periodically
- **Cost:** 8 Gold
- **Rarity:** Unique
- **Description:** Autonomous bot that adapts to battle conditions
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Gingerbread Jerry

### DDoS Protection Module
- **Shape:** 3x2 (6 slots)
- **Effect:** On hit: 50% chance to stun for 0.5s. Gains Heat at Fatigue start
- **Cost:** 8 Gold
- **Rarity:** Unique
- **Description:** Advanced protection against distributed attacks
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Pumpkin

### Cryogenic Cooling System
- **Shape:** 2x2 (4 slots square)
- **Effect:** Inflicts Cold and increases damage under certain conditions
- **Cost:** 6 Gold
- **Rarity:** Unique
- **Description:** Extreme cooling that slows enemy processes
- **Backpack Battles Equivalent:** Snowcake
